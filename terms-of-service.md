# Terms of Service — fivestarsclips

Last updated: 15 November 2026

## 1. What this is
fivestarsclips is a self-hosted clipping tool. It is used by its owner to turn live streams — the owner's own streams and streams by other creators — into short, edited highlight clips (cut to the best moments, reframed to vertical, with added captions/subtitles, titles and commentary), and to publish them to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook through those platforms' official APIs.

## 2. Who may use it
The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner uses it, with their own accounts.

## 3. Content from other creators
- Clips of other creators' streams are only published when the original creator has given permission (for example through a clipping program or direct agreement), or where the use is otherwise permitted by law (such as commentary or other transformative use).
- Every clip credits the original creator (name and channel) in the caption.
- Any creator can ask for their clips to be removed by contacting the address below; such requests are handled promptly, and the clips are taken down.
- Content is never presented as the owner's own when it is not.

## 4. Responsibility
The owner is responsible for everything published, including rights to the footage, music, game content and people appearing in it, and for following each platform's terms, community guidelines and copyright rules.

## 5. Platform rules
Publishing happens only through official APIs (such as TikTok's Content Posting API), with the account owner's explicit consent. The app does not automate browsers, use cookies, or access accounts in any unofficial way.
