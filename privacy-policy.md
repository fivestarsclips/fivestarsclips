# Privacy Policy — fivestarsclips

Last updated: 15 November 2026

fivestarsclips is a self-hosted tool used by its owner to edit highlight clips from live streams (their own and  other creators') and publish them to their own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook). It is not offered to the public.

## What data the app accesses
When the owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives, with the owner's consent:
- Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) — scope `user.info.basic`.
- Permission to upload and publish videos to that account — scopes `video.upload` and `video.publish`.
- Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok.

The app does not collect personal data from viewers or from the creators whose streams are clipped.

## How the data is used
- Profile information is only used to show which account is connected.
- Tokens are only used to upload the videos the owner chooses to publish, with the caption and privacy settings the owner selects.
- Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

## Storage and security
- All data is stored only on the owner's own computer/server, never on third-party servers.
- Tokens are encrypted at rest.
- The app never asks for or stores passwords.

## Retention and deletion
- Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens immediately.
- Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
